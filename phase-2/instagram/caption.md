## Caption (EN)

WEIDE — Longevity & Regenerative Health.

Weide is German for willow: a tree that grows again from a single cutting, whose bark gave medicine salicin, and that bends in the storm without breaking. We built the identity of a premium longevity house in Austria on that story.

A woven W like willow rods. Serifs that touch to form one line. A calm palette of Bitter Cocoa, Weathered Glass and Cayenne Dust.

Time, well kept.
